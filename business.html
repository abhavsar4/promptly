Revenue Model

PROMPTly is highly scalable due to low-cost manufacturing utilizing standard hygiene product supply chains paired with high-value microfluidic integration.

  • Manufacturing Cost: ~$0.15 per unit
  • Target Retail Price:
    • $35.99 per 21-pack (one-week supply)
    • $149.99 per 90-pack (one-month supply)
  • Healthy Margins: 91% gross margin at scale, allowing for significant reinvestment into R&D, clinical trials, and international expansion.

The Insurance Pitch

We are shifting the financial burden from a high-cost acute setting (the ER) to a low-cost preventative setting (the home).

  • Cost of Inaction: >$1,800 for an unnecessary ER visit.
  • Cost of Prevention: $149.99 for a PROMPTly Pack.
  • The Result: Insurers save over $1,650 per avoided false alarm while preventing catastrophic preterm complications.

Go-To-Market Timeline

1

Phase 1: Direct-to-Consumer (DTC)

Initial launch via e-commerce targeting high-risk, anxious mothers. Generates early revenue to fund extensive multi-center clinical trials. Our OB/GYN partners recommend PROMPTly to their patients, earning a small commission and reducing their own clinical workload.

2

Phase 2: Clinical Trials & Conferences

With our revenue stream established, we begin multi-center prospective, paired-comparison clinical trials demonstrating non-inferiority against the AmniSure gold standard at a lower cost. This data, along with our historical sales data and voluntary registry, provides evidence to present at clinical conferences such as the American College of Obstetrics & Gynecology (ACOG), elevating our standing in the medical community and expanding our pool of physician partners.

3

Phase 3: Broad Insurance & International

Our clinical data grants us HCPCS diagnostic reimbursement codes to unlock national scale, followed by expansion into high-GNI international markets.
